//03 AI receptionist (Nova)

What is Nova AI?+

Nova is our 24/7 AI receptionist. It answers every call, books appointments, answers common questions, takes messages, and routes urgent calls to a person.

Does it sound robotic?+

No. Nova uses natural, conversational speech, handles interruptions, and responds in under two seconds. Most callers don’t realize they’re speaking with AI unless Nova tells them.

What happens when a caller needs a human?+

Nova transfers live to the right person or team based on your rules, and sends a summary of the conversation so nobody starts over.

How long does setup take?+

About five business days. We learn your business, configure Nova, test it with you, and go live.

Can it handle many calls at once?+

Yes. Nova answers unlimited concurrent calls, so nobody hears a busy signal or waits on hold.

Is it HIPAA-compliant?+

Yes. Nova can be deployed with HIPAA-compliant handling for medical and dental practices, including signed BAAs.

A Chief AI Officer on a part-time retainer. You get executive-level AI strategy, vendor selection, and governance for a fraction of a $250K+ salary.
